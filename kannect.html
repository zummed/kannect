Kannect

Choose a wall

Every wall is open. Tap any number to play it.

How to play
  1. Sixteen tiles hide four groups of four. Tap four tiles you think belong together.
  2. A correct group locks in at the top. Some tiles fit more than one group, so a group that looks right can still be wrong.
  3. When all four groups are sorted, say what links each one.
  4. Pictures swaps some words for pictures. Work out what each picture stands for; it may not be what it first looks like.
  5. There is no timer and no limit on guesses.
